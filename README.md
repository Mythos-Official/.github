# Mythos

Mythos builds technology to help people develop a healthier relationship with the devices they use every day. We started with a question: **Can headphones encourage safer listening without sacrificing sound or comfort?**

Our first prototype, **Mythos Dream**, began in an engineering design class. Four high school students had noticed the effects of long listening sessions on their hearing and set out to improve the experience. They combined psychoacoustic techniques with cooling ear cushions to make extended listening more comfortable while encouraging safer listening levels.

We’re now developing the next iteration of Mythos Dream, with active noise cancellation, an improved audio safety algorithm, and a sleeker design.
